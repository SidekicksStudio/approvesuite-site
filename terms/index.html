2. The Service

Approve Suite is a regulatory monitoring and research tool for the life-science industry. It collects records that United States government agencies and public registries publish, currently including FDA warning letters, inspection classifications, recalls and enforcement reports, Federal Register documents, Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Drugs@FDA approvals and complete response letters, the FDA Purple Book, ClinicalTrials.gov and SEC EDGAR filings. It uses software and AI models to organize, label and summarize those records, builds company profiles from them, lets you ask questions about them ("Ask AI"), and delivers the items that match the watchlist you configure by dashboard, email digest and email alert.

The plans, their prices and what each includes are described on the pricing page at the time you sign up, and the limits each plan carries are set out in section 9. The sources we cover, and how often each is read, change as agencies change what and how they publish.
