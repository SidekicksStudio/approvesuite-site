2. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your email address and password, and optionally your name, company name, your role (for example quality or regulatory, or vendor), and time zone. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a Google account identifier from Google instead of a password. When you choose a password, we check it against a public list of breached passwords (Have I Been Pwned) by sending only the first five characters of a one-way hash of it; the password itself and your identity never leave our servers. We record whether you ticked the marketing consent box at sign-up, the wording you saw, and when.

Watchlists and delivery settings

The companies, indications, CFR parts, GxP areas, and keywords you add to a watchlist, the saved views you create, your digest schedule, and the email addresses you ask us to deliver digests to. We treat watchlist contents as confidential: they are visible only to you and are never shown to other customers. Each delivery address you add receives a verification email and is used only after its owner confirms it.

Ask AI conversations

The questions you type, any text you highlighted to ask about, the page and filters you were viewing, the answers, and the database lookups the model ran to produce them. Conversations are stored on our servers so you can return to them; you can rename or delete any conversation, and all of them are deleted with your account. We also record token counts and cost per answer to enforce plan limits.

Feedback

Whether you marked an item useful or noise, any note you attached, and any product feedback you send us (with the page you sent it from).

Billing information

If you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment card details directly; we never see or store the card number. We store your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, plan, billing interval, status, and billing period dates.

Newsletter subscription

If you subscribe to our weekly newsletter, we store your email address, where you signed up (for example the footer or the weekly page), the consent sentence you saw, when you confirmed, and when you unsubscribed. If you vote on an issue, we store the vote.

Email delivery data

Whether an email we sent was delivered, bounced, or was marked as spam, so we can stop sending to addresses that do not want or cannot receive our email.

Usage, analytics, and attribution data

Page views and feature interactions (for example a sign-up, viewing pricing, starting checkout, or asking an AI question), and how you first found us: the referring website, campaign tags (UTM parameters), an advertising click identifier if you arrived from an ad, the first page you landed on, and which version of our landing page headline you were shown, because we test variations. Section 5 lists the tools involved and section 6 explains your choices.

Technical data and logs

Your IP address, browser type, and the pages and API endpoints you request, held briefly in server logs and in the counters we use for rate limiting and abuse prevention. When something breaks, an error report with technical context is sent to our error-monitoring provider (section 7).

Support correspondence

Emails you send to us, and our replies.

What we do not collect

The regulatory records shown in the Service come from public government sources, not from you. We do not ask for, and you should not enter, patient data, unpublished regulatory submissions, or other confidential company documents, including in Ask AI. We do not collect precise location data, and we do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by California law.

  • To provide, operate, and improve Approve Suite, including matching public records against your watchlist, sending your digests and alerts, and answering your Ask AI questions
  • To authenticate your account, enforce plan and AI limits, and keep the Service secure and free of abuse
  • To process payments via Stripe and keep the records that tax and accounting law require
  • To send account, security, and billing emails, the onboarding emails that follow sign-up, and the weekly email that comes with a free account
  • To send product updates, tips, and offers only if you asked for them, with a one-click unsubscribe in every message
  • To respond to support requests
  • To understand how the Service is used and which pages and campaigns bring people to it, and to measure whether our advertising works
  • To comply with law and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not use your watchlists or conversations to train AI models.

Lawful basis for processing (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases: contract (to deliver the Service you signed up for), legitimate interests (to improve the product, understand how it is used, prevent abuse, ensure security, and measure our marketing, balanced against your rights), legal obligation (tax and accounting records), and consent (optional marketing emails and advertising cookies, which you may withdraw at any time).

4. AI processing

We use AI models from Anthropic (United States) in three ways. First, to classify and summarize the public regulatory records the Service collects; no personal data of yours is involved. Second, to write the short introduction of your digest, for which the model receives the name of your watchlist and which of your rules each item matched. Third, to answer Ask AI questions, for which the model receives what section 2 describes under "Ask AI conversations." It never receives your name, email address, or payment details. Under our agreement with Anthropic, the data we send is not used to train its models. We do not read your conversations except to investigate abuse, a security incident, or a support request you make.

6. Analytics, advertising, and your privacy choices

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website and app are used: which pages are viewed, which features are used, and which campaigns and referrers bring people who go on to sign up or subscribe. Events carry the first-visit attribution described in section 2 and, when you subscribe, the plan and price; the purchase event also carries a pseudonymous account identifier so the report can count subscriptions exactly once. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses; it uses them only to derive a coarse location. Google keeps this data for up to 14 months. Vercel Web Analytics counts page views and referrers in aggregate with no identifier for you. We also keep our own count of first visits by campaign, referrer domain and landing page; a row in it holds no identifier for you and no IP address.

Advertising measurement and audiences. We use the Google Ads tag to measure whether an ad led to a sign-up or subscription and, when a campaign runs, to build audiences for showing our ads to people who visited the site. Google may combine this with data from your Google account under its own privacy policy. Google Tag Manager may load similar pixels from other advertising platforms we advertise on. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act this kind of measurement counts as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, even though no money changes hands and we never hand over your name or email.

Your choices. Use the control below to opt out. It stops the advertising cookies and audience sharing in this browser; usage analytics stays on so the product keeps working and we can see what to fix. We also honor the Global Privacy Control signal: a browser or extension that sends it is treated as opted out without a click. We do not respond to the older "Do Not Track" setting, which has no agreed meaning. You can also opt out of Google's personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com and of Google Analytics everywhere with Google's browser add-on.

[The opt-out control is not shown on this copy of the policy. While Approve Suite is paused, this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising tags.]

9. Data retention

  • Account, watchlists, saved views, conversations, and feedback: for as long as your account is active. Conversations you delete are removed at once.
  • Copies of digests and delivery records: up to one year.
  • Raw copies of fetched public records, pipeline run logs, and server logs: up to 90 days.
  • Rate-limit counters: minutes.
  • Error reports (Sentry): up to 90 days.
  • Google Analytics: up to 14 months. Vercel Web Analytics holds aggregate counts only.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. We keep the address of an unsubscribed or bounced recipient so we do not email it again.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law require, typically seven years, held by Stripe and in our records.

If you delete your account, your subscription is canceled and your profile, watchlists, delivery settings, saved views, conversations, and feedback are deleted immediately; copies in our database backups expire within 30 days. We keep only what the law requires us to keep (payment records) and the suppression records above.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS with HSTS), a strict content security policy, row-level security in our database so only you can read your watchlists and conversations, hashed passwords screened against known breaches, signed webhooks, rate limiting, and access controls on administrative functions. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and tell us at info@approvesuite.com if you suspect unauthorized access.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information (your profile settings let you do most of this yourself)
  • Deletion: request that we delete your account and associated data (profile settings do this at once)
  • Portability: receive your watchlists and settings in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction: request that we limit processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests, including analytics
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent (marketing emails, advertising cookies), withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing, using the unsubscribe link or section 6
  • Opt out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising: section 6

You can edit or delete watchlists and conversations at any time and close your account from your profile settings. For other requests, contact us at info@approvesuite.com from the address on your account, or tell us how to verify that you are the person the data is about; we will only use what you give us to verify the request. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission. We respond within 30 days (EEA and UK) or 45 days (United States), extendable once where the law allows, and we will tell you if we need more time. If we decline a request, we will say why, and residents of U.S. states that provide it may appeal by replying to our decision; if the appeal is denied you may contact your state attorney general.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.
